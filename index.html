System rules

Daily quest. Every day has four objectives: focused minutes, DSA problems, at least two different skills, and job hunt actions on weekdays. Clear all four and the day counts.

Streak. A cleared day adds one. Every 7th day in a row earns a shield (you can hold two).

Missed day. The day closes at midnight. If it was not cleared, a shield is burned. With no shield: the streak resets to zero, you lose 200 XP (levels can drop), and a penalty is added.

Penalty. Each penalty adds 60 minutes to the next daily quest, up to two. Clearing a day wipes the penalty.

XP. Study and revision: 2 XP a minute. Mock interview: 3 XP a minute. Problems: 20 / 40 / 80 XP for easy / medium / hard, plus 1 XP a minute. Job hunt action: 15 XP. Node lit in a skill tree: 50 XP. A DSA node counts as a solved problem instead. Daily clear: 100 XP plus 5 per streak day (up to 100 extra).

Revision cycles. When every node of the DSA tree or the System Design tree is lit, you get 500 XP and the whole tree goes dark again for the next revision cycle.

Ranks. E from level 1, D from 8, C from 16, B from 28, A from 42, S from 60.

No cheating yourself. Entries need a real note. You cannot log yesterday. An entry can be undone for ten minutes, then it is permanent.
